R package

rsvarnet

Robust regime-switching VAR networks for electricity prices: estimation, debiased inference, multi-step forecasting and simulation for high-dimensional VAR-X models via ℓ1-penalized adaptive Huber regression. Companion to the Nordic bidding-zones paper.

Nordic Price Calculator

An interactive companion tool to my regime-switching VAR research. Enter today's electricity prices across the eleven Nordic bidding zones and forecast tomorrow's, conditional on the announced grid state. For academic illustration only.
