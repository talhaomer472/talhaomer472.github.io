About

I am a Senior Lecturer in Statistics in the Department of Economics and Statistics at Linnaeus University, Sweden, holding a PhD in Statistics from Jönköping University (2024).

My research focuses on econometrics and statistical learning for mixed-frequency and high-dimensional models, shrinkage and regularization methods, biostatistics and statistical methods for health and disease modeling, and macro-financial forecasting — with applications including volatility forecasting and health-related count-data modeling.

Before Linnaeus I was a Research Fellow at Jönköping University, contributing to the project "Improving the Swedish Regulatory Model for Electricity Distribution." I have taught statistics and econometrics across Sweden and Pakistan at undergraduate, master, and PhD levels.
